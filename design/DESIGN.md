# bro — Grounded Editorial

The current visual authority is [the Grounded Editorial guide](mens-health-design-guide.md), including its dark appearance extension, and [its exact tokens](mens-health-design-tokens.json). The September 2026 handoff supersedes the Helm visual rules below. The PDF is visual reference; its fictional values and proposed fields are not product defaults.

- Keep Journal / Intake / Body / Life, Headings, existing routes, scale versions and record identity.
- Off-white canvas with darker warm stone surfaces in light appearance; warm charcoal and sage in dark. System is the default for fresh installs. Persist explicit appearance choices.
- Brand is teal/sage for selection and primary actions. Clay is reserved for + Log. Personal-history bands are neutral, with no clinical meaning. No glow or chart hatching.
- Caladea Regular display and title styles, Caladea Regular notes; sans-serif UI, measurements, units and chart labels. Tabular numerals. Page inset 24; cards 16; controls 12; sheets 24; buttons 52; touch targets at least 48.
- Five equal check-in choices. Selection updates a draft; Continue advances; Save for now commits the answered dimensions. Never turn an unanswered score into zero. Preserve the existing Energy and Motivation scale meanings.
- Completed records show their event date/time and source. Charts use actual records and preserve gaps and the existing personal-range calculation. Provide named values and reading lists.
- Retain factual, non-judgmental copy and existing privacy, provenance, archive and deletion boundaries. Never add clinical ratings, calorie allowances, compensatory exercise, streaks or a combined Life score.
- Both appearances share components and layout; keep values, drafts and navigation when appearance changes. Honour reduced motion and accessible control states.

Native implementation and the component mapping are in [REACT_NATIVE.md](REACT_NATIVE.md).

The superseded Helm package — its rulebook, component spec, tokens and HTML studies — is kept under [archive/helm/](archive/helm/) as historical context. Nothing in it governs new work.
